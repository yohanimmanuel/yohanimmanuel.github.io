# Welcome to the Grid

Personal portfolio of Yohan Immanuel Hendrajaya, a product developer and full-stack builder based in Singapore. The site is a static site: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no build step and no server.

It presents work that connects operations, data, and people. Freight platforms, logistics tools, analytics, and product systems sit next to a short profile of experience, education, and the stack behind them.

## Pages

- **Home** introduces the portfolio and highlights selected work.
- **About** covers work experience, education, and languages. Roles include Product Developer at Beiling Pesona Abadi, Business Data Analyst at COSCO SHIPPING, and Research Intern at Universitas Ciputra Surabaya. Studies span SIM, the University of London, Singapore Polytechnic, and the National University of Singapore.
- **Projects** walks through seven builds, including an AI WhatsApp customer service agent, a logistics control tower and driver app, FreightLynk, a missile projectile defence simulation, University of London data science work, Unreal Engine 5 architectural visualization, and hand-gesture control in Python.
- **Stack** lists the tools used across web and mobile, AI and machine learning, backend, databases, real-time systems, CI/CD, and containers.

The first visit opens a short terminal gate. After that, the same browser session skips it. Project and portrait images open in a gallery.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The pages link to each other with relative paths, so the folder can also be served by any static host.

Enjoy!
