01 · WHATSAPP

AI 24-hour Customer Service Agent

MAR 2026 - APR 2026

This project is a responsive AI customer service agent on WhatsApp that handles buyer messaging and extends conversations into actionable sales execution. It understands buyer intent, responds to product and order inquiries, and can assign relevant sales jobs to sales representatives when human follow-up is required.

Built as a focused feature, the system connects conversational AI with business context and sales workflows, moving beyond customer support toward AI-assisted commerce and sales operations. A conversation can leave the usual support path, inside a regulated sandbox, according to the rules and requirements the user sets.

02 · OPERATIONS

Logistics Control Tower & Driver App

FEB 2026 - MAR 2026

This project is a logistics operational control tower, where real-time delivery operations are centralized into a single system for monitoring, coordination, and execution. Dispatchers and drivers manage delivery assignments, track shipment progress, update delivery milestones, and keep visibility across ongoing operations.

03 · ENGINEERING SIMULATION

Missile Projectile Defence System

DEC 2025 - FEB 2026

Designed and developed a missile projectile defence system engineering project, focused on threat detection, tracking, and interception logic. The system simulates incoming projectile trajectories and applies decision-making algorithms to judge interception feasibility and response timing.

  • Projectile motion modeling and trajectory prediction using physics-based equations
  • Target tracking and classification based on speed, direction, and altitude
  • Interception logic to calculate response paths and engagement timing
  • System-level design emphasizing accuracy, latency, and reliability
  • Simulation and performance evaluation under multiple threat scenarios

04 · UNIVERSITY OF LONDON

ST2195 Programming for Data Science

NOV 2025 - DEC 2025

University of London data science project covering simulation and a large historical flight dataset.

  • Simulated and computed a Markov Chain Monte Carlo algorithm, running a random-walk Metropolis sampler with a sigmoid equation and reporting the R-hat value
  • Computed and analyzed a 10 million flight dataset from the 2009 ASA Statistical Computing and Graphics Data Expo (Harvard) to study delays caused by a mix of scheduling, operational congestion, and aircraft characteristics
  • Used graphical analysis and machine learning, including a logistic regression model with a confusion matrix, to predict expected flight delays and diversions

05 · SAAS · PT. BEILING PESONA ABADI

FreightLynk

DEC 2024 - APR 2025

A freight management system built for PT. Beiling Pesona Abadi. It reached 56 importers around Surabaya and improved the search for reliable relationships while avoiding untrusted buyers and fraudulent actions. This was the initial startup project, exited and bought by PT. Trust Trading Indonesia in two years' time. The product is a cargo booking system with live cargo tracking and online document submission.

  • AI-enabled sourcing
  • Categorization and filtering for finding buyers or suppliers
  • Freight management workflows for operations

Associated with the IS2182 Innovating Digital Systems and Services coursework, extended to suit BPA's air and sea freight process.

07 · TOUCHLESS UI

Hand Gesture Recognition & Automation using Python

DEC 2023 - FEB 2024
